# V4 release preparation, 2026-09-08

Rescue v2 was deleted at the owner's request: local branch
`feature/zero-result-rescue` and its `zero-result-rescue` worktree are gone.
It was never merged into main or enabled in production.

V4 capture had a reproducibility defect: identical concurrent scanner requests
were recorded in completion order. Replaying those responses in invocation
order could change candidate payoff nodes. Capture now preserves invocation
order and matching provider IDs, snapshots request phase before awaiting the
provider, and reuses the same recorder for recovery calls. Two regressions
reproduced reversed responses before the fix and passed after it. Old scratch
recordings made in completion order are not release evidence; recapture them.
Freshness tests now use a fixed clock aligned with their dated fixtures.

Validation: 300 tests across 12 suites, worker typecheck, and worker/shared
build passed in Docker. Eleven historical empty jobs with retained media
represented three distinct videos. Sources and transcripts were inspected;
new real provider responses were captured with the corrected production helper.
All three captures replayed in off/shadow/on: nine checks passed with exact
request fingerprints and no additional output. Successful recovery and rejection
paths are covered by the integration tests; real recovery benefit remains unproven.

A development-only experiment changing the critic's value rubric was rejected:
it also admitted an introductory Windows definition. No prompt or quality
threshold changes are included in this release.

Private source files, captures, and replay results are under ignored
`apps/worker/.corpus/v4-release-2026-09-08/`. Customer rows and deliveries were
not changed by these checks.

Activation is pending the owner's explicit decision about the existing release
criterion: four recoverable and four valid-empty cases, including holdout,
and at least two real recoveries. The current outcome corpus does not meet it.
Do not describe technical tests as a passing outcome quality gate.
The prepared activation changes only `ANALYZE_OUTCOME_RECOVERY_V1=shadow` to
`on`, with at most six candidates and the existing shared quality checks.
`ANALYZE_PUBLISHABILITY=on` remains active. Rollback is recovery `shadow` plus
recreation of the analyze worker after its active queue has drained.
